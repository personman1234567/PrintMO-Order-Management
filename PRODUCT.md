# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

PrintMO's owners are the primary users. They manage orders throughout a busy print-shop workday, sometimes from a computer and sometimes from a phone across the room. The interface is embedded within Shopify Admin, so it must remain usable inside Shopify's available viewport rather than assuming a full-screen browser.

Their job is to understand every order's current stage, see what needs attention next, reference the customer's mockups and print-placement details, batch blank-apparel purchasing, and move work through production without manually reconciling information across systems.

## Product Purpose

PrintMO Order Management is the operational source of truth for the shop's order-production workflow. It brings Shopify orders, workflow stages, S&S blank-apparel purchasing, customer mockups, print-placement details, and production progress into one trackable workspace.

Success means preventing missed, stalled, or incorrectly staged orders while reducing manual sorting and processing time. Desktop and mobile are equally important: both viewport versions must provide the complete workflow, not a reduced mobile companion.

## Positioning

PrintMO's complete order-production workflow in one clear, trackable workspace.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, efficient, and intuitive. The interface should communicate the right operational information with strong hierarchy and minimal visual noise, remaining easy to understand during a busy production day.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard conventions that are disconnected from PrintMO's real workflow. Avoid visual density that becomes noisy or overwhelming. The product must feel purpose-built for how the shop runs while remaining modular enough to incorporate relevant new workflow stages, information, and actions.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the next action obvious.** Order state, urgency, blockers, and available actions should be understandable without hunting through the interface.
2. **Keep one operational truth.** Mockups, print-placement details, purchasing state, production progress, and fulfillment state should remain connected to the order rather than forcing manual cross-checking.
3. **Preserve complete capability across viewports.** Mobile is a full production surface inside Shopify Admin, not a simplified read-only version of desktop.
4. **Show consequences and feedback.** State-changing actions must communicate what will happen, confirm what happened, and support safe recovery wherever practical.
5. **Expand through the workflow, not around it.** New stages and operational details should extend a coherent system instead of accumulating as unrelated panels or generic cards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline and exceed it when doing so improves practical usability. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, logical reading and tab order, reduced-motion support, clear non-color state cues, and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. Mobile layouts must remain operable inside Shopify Admin's constrained viewport and under the distraction and movement of an active shop environment.
